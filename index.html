How to Prepare Effectively

1
Familiarize with Format

Technical papers often contain multiple sections or specialized drawings. Familiarize yourself with the layout and structure before the actual test.

2
Study Official Memorandums

Use the marking guidelines (Memorandums) to self-assess your mock answers. Pay close attention to steps required in calculations and drawing keys.

3
Target Repeated Topics

Look for questions that appear consistently every year. Ensure you have fully mastered these core concepts and their application.
